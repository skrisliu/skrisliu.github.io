Shengjie Kris Liu

Remote Sensing, Deep Learning, AI for Earth

I study the physical world, as a living planet, via computational representation.

People are embedded within this world, shaping and being shaped by it through everyday activities. Satellites, sensors, and other observation systems generate massive yet sparse and noisy data. My research aims to understand the physical world from imperfect data, to predict its structure and dynamics, and ultimately to produce actionable information that improves how people interact with the world we live in.

My work has received awards and recognition from AGU and IEEE and has been supported by projects and centers funded through NSF, NIH, and the Environment and Conservation Fund. I did my PhD at USC in California. Before that I spent two years at the University of Hong Kong. In 2025 I was a summer fellow at the Center for Learning the Earth with AI and Physics (LEAP) at Columbia University.
